Vice Frame 3 turns your own photos into bold multi-panel poster cover art in the spirit of classic game covers: six collage layouts with white keylines, six colour grades — and, new in this version, a different grade on every panel, so a collage can clash teal against magenta the way real covers do. It ships with five original illustrated poster scenes drawn procedurally on canvas at 1920 × 1200 — a sunset pier with a lowrider, a downtown skyline at dusk, a neon boulevard, a palm-lined highway with a sports car, and a beach day — alongside two bundled photographs. Add the rotatable starburst badge, the bottom banner strip and the magazine Cover Lines layout, hit **Surprise me** when you want a curated look dealt for you, and export a print-ready PNG. The app runs entirely offline in your browser; your pictures never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

## How it works

- **Layouts** — Vice Grid, Slash, Triptych, Mosaic, Poster and the new magazine-style Cover Lines (a hero panel with headline strips down the side rail) arrange your pictures into keylined panels that adapt to every export aspect.
- **Pictures** — use the built-in illustrated scenes and photos, or add your own by file picker or drag & drop; click a panel to select it, drag to reposition its picture, scroll or use the slider to zoom, and deal one photo across every panel if you like.
- **Per-panel grades** — Sunset, Neon Nights (a true split-tone: teal shadows, magenta highlights), Golden Hour, Miami Pastel, Noir and Raw can be mixed panel by panel from each panel's own Grade menu, then finished with sunburst rays, halftone dots, a light leak, vignette and grain.
- **Caption & furniture** — two editable lines in Anton with Sunset, Neon, Chrome or Candy lettering, a size slider, top or bottom placement and an optional poster slant; a starburst sticker with editable text and a rotation slider; and an optional bottom banner strip with its own line.
- **Surprise me** — one click deals a curated look: layout, scene mix, harmonising per-panel grades, lettering and furniture that are known to sit well together.
- **History** — undo and redo buttons (or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z) step back through every change.
- **Export** — one click downloads a PNG at 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080, rendered by the same pipeline as the live preview.
